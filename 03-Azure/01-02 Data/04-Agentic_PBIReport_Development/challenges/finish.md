# Finish - Show the evidence

[Home](README.md) | [Previous](challenge-03.md)

Demonstrate the full chain: chosen CSV version and checksums, two successful
loader executions, model reconciliation, and a rendered report iteration. Explain
one agent mistake that human review caught. Never claim that deployment success,
static JSON checks or attractive screenshots alone prove correct numbers.

## Reflection and optional extensions

Compare your instructions with [team guidance](../resources/copilot-instructions.md).
Optionally ask Copilot to document measures or propose RLS. RLS needs a separate,
appropriately licensed consumer identity and effective-role tests; workspace admins
are not suitable proof of consumer restrictions. These are extensions, not a
fourth required challenge.

## Cleanup and ownership

Follow the shared [cleanup responsibilities](../resources/cleanup.md) with your
coach. Only remove resources when instructed; deleting an Azure resource group
does not delete a Fabric workspace.
