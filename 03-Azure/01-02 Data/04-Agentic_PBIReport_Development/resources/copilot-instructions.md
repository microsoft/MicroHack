# Suggested exercise-folder instructions

Copy/adapt these instructions in your **own** exercise folder after review.
They are guidance, not authorization or an access-control boundary.

- Plan before writes, name the target identity/workspace and show the proposed
  item changes. Request human approval for deployment, execution and deletion.
- Use only this lab's wholly synthetic dataset and supplied capacity. Do not
  create capacity, licenses, identities or unrelated Fabric services.
- Inspect available host tools, model availability and permissions. Never claim
  an operation ran because source was generated. Surface terminal errors.
- Keep secrets, live IDs, local settings, logs and screenshots outside Git.
- Preserve explicit schemas, grains, PK/FK checks and source hashes. Never silently
  coerce malformed values, drop rows or sum stock snapshots across dates.
- Use a declared as-of date independent of filtered fact availability. Align
  insurance periods; distinguish reserve movements, balances and claim activity.
- Preserve model/report source and reviewable diffs. Execute reconciliation queries
  and inspect rendered visuals. Label unsupported/missing evidence honestly.
- Manual Fabric experimentation is permitted. Allowed item types are an organizer
  policy concern, not something these instructions or a Contributor role enforce.
