# SOP-CMP-001 Air compressor

- **Document:** SOP-CMP-001
- **Revision:** B (effective 2026-02-05)
- **Applies to:** compressor CMP-01
- **Owner:** Utilities team
- **Related error code:** E-CMP-107 (discharge temperature high)

## Limits

- Normal discharge temperature: 80 to 95 °C.
- E-CMP-107 is raised above **100 °C**. The compressor trips at 110 °C.
- Normal working pressure: 7.5 bar.

## E-CMP-107: discharge temperature high

1. Check the compressor room. The room temperature must be below 40 °C and the ventilation openings must be free.
2. Complete SOP-SAF-001, and release all stored air pressure before you open any cover.
3. Check the oil level. Top up with oil **CMP-OIL-46** up to the middle of the sight glass.
4. Clean the cooler fins with compressed air from the inside to the outside.
5. If the temperature is still above 100 °C after cleaning, create a high-priority ticket. Do not run the compressor above 105 °C.

## Routine

- Drain the condensate every day.
- Replace the oil and the oil filter every **2000 operating hours**.
- Replace the air filter every 4000 operating hours.
