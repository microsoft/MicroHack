# SOP-CNC-002 CNC coolant system

- **Document:** SOP-CNC-002
- **Revision:** B (effective 2025-11-20)
- **Applies to:** CNC-03, CNC-07, LATHE-05
- **Owner:** Maintenance Engineering
- **Related error code:** E-CLT-042 (coolant level low)

## Coolant specification

- Coolant type: water-miscible emulsion, product code CLT-8.
- Mixing ratio: **8 percent** concentrate. Check with the refractometer. The acceptable range is 7 to 9 percent.
- Tank temperature must stay below 35 °C.

## E-CLT-042: coolant level low

1. Stop the program at the end of the current cycle. Do not stop a cut in the middle.
2. Complete SOP-SAF-001 if you must open any cover.
3. Fill the tank with premixed coolant up to the MAX mark. Never add pure concentrate or pure water directly to the tank.
4. Check the filter. Replace the filter if the filter differential pressure is above 1.5 bar.
5. Check the lines and the fittings for leaks. Create a ticket if you find a leak.

## Weekly tasks

- Measure the concentration and the pH. The target pH is 8.5 to 9.5.
- Remove tramp oil from the surface.
- Replace the whole tank content every 6 months, or when it smells bad.
