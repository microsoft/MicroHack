# SOP-CNV-001 Belt conveyor

- **Document:** SOP-CNV-001
- **Revision:** A (effective 2025-09-12)
- **Applies to:** belt conveyor CONV-03
- **Owner:** Maintenance Engineering
- **Related error code:** E-CNV-009 (motor overload)

## Motor current

- Normal motor current: 8 to 12 A.
- E-CNV-009 is raised when the motor current is above **14 A** for 5 seconds.

## E-CNV-009: motor overload

1. Complete SOP-SAF-001.
2. Look for a jam, a stuck roller or product on the belt, and clear it.
3. Turn the rollers by hand. Replace any roller that does not turn freely.
4. Check the belt tracking. The belt edge must stay within 10 mm of the centre line. Adjust the tail pulley in steps of a quarter turn.
5. Check the belt tension. The belt may sag by at most 15 mm in the middle of a span.

## Lubrication

Grease the drive and tail bearings every **500 operating hours** with grease **NLGI 2**. Use 3 pumps of the grease gun for each bearing. Do not over-grease.
