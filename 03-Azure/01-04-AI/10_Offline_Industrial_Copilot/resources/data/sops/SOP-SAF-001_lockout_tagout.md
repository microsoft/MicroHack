# SOP-SAF-001 Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) before maintenance

- **Document:** SOP-SAF-001
- **Revision:** D (effective 2026-03-01)
- **Applies to:** all machines at Contoso Precision Machining
- **Owner:** Plant Safety Officer

## Purpose

No maintenance task may start until the machine is in a zero-energy state. This SOP applies before every task described in any other SOP.

## Steps

1. Tell the shift supervisor and the machine operator that you are about to isolate the machine.
2. Stop the machine using the normal stop procedure. Do not use the emergency stop to stop a running machine, except in an emergency.
3. Switch the main isolator to OFF and fit your **personal red lock** and a danger tag with your name and the date.
4. Isolate every other energy source: compressed air (release the stored pressure), hydraulic pressure (open the bleed valve), and stored mechanical energy (lower or block raised parts).
5. **Verify the zero-energy state.** Try to start the machine from the control panel. The machine must not start. Return the controls to OFF.
6. Only the person who fitted a lock may remove it. If that person has left the site, call the Plant Safety Officer. Never cut a lock without written approval.

## Rules

- One lock per person. Group work needs a lock box and one lock per person on the box.
- Work on a spindle, a hydraulic circuit or a pressure vessel also needs a second person present as a **safety observer**.
- Keep hands out of any machine area until step 5 has passed.
