## 🎯 Objective

Estimate what your agents would save Caldova, decide what it would take to use them for real, and leave your lab tidy.

## ✅ Tasks

### Part A: Measure the impact (10 min)

The case overview expects **30–50% less time** spent monitoring and interpreting change, and **15–25% less remediation** effort. Estimate both for Caldova this week:

| Activity this week | By hand (your estimate) | With your agents | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finding and reading the 10 publications, including the duplicate and the revision | | | Monitor run summaries |
| Working out what applies and linking it to products, labels, studies, SOPs and quality records | | | `impact-agent` answers |
| Answering staff questions and drafting emails, change requests and label updates | | | `qa-agent` answers |
| Preparing the leadership briefing and tracking actions | | | `briefing-agent`, task register |

Which issue would most likely have been found **late** without the agents? (Hint: look at the warning letter, the PSUR timeline and the trial summary.)

### Part B: From training to production (10 min)

Discuss as a team:

1. **Real sources.** The monitor reads fictional portals. What changes for real regulator and payer websites: terms of use, rate limits, PDFs, login walls and page redesigns?
2. **Trust.** Who checks the agents' work, and how often? What would you measure in Foundry evaluations?
3. **Records.** Where would the task register and audit trail live in your organisation: a quality management system or a regulatory information management system?
4. **People.** Which decisions must always stay with a qualified person?
