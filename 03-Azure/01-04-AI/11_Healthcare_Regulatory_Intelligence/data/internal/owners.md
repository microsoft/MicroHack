# Owners and internal due dates

Fictional training record. Owner: Regulatory Affairs Lead. Last reviewed: 2 January 2027.

Caldova uses roles, not personal names, in this register.

## Who owns which domain

Caldova records every task under one of five domains: **clinical**, **labelling**, **manufacturing**, **vigilance** or **market access**.

| Domain | Owner role | Typical work |
|---|---|---|
| Labelling | Labelling Lead | Label text, translations and artwork: the content of a label change |
| Vigilance (pharmacovigilance and post-market surveillance) | Pharmacovigilance Lead | Safety reporting, PSURs, risk management plans, DHPCs |
| Clinical | Clinical Disclosure Lead | Trial registration and results disclosure |
| Manufacturing and quality | Quality Head | Sites, contract manufacturers, CAPAs, deviations, batch release |
| Market access | Market Access Lead | Payer dossiers, coverage, pricing schemes, forecasts |
| Submissions (not a domain: record the task under the domain of the change, for example labelling) | Regulatory Affairs Lead | Preparing and submitting variations and supplements; contact with regulators |

## Approval

The **Regulatory Affairs Lead** approves the action plan for every new regulatory change. The **Quality Head** must also approve any action that affects product release or supply.

## How Caldova sets internal due dates

**Today** is the *Status as of* date at the top of `tracking/task-register.md`.

Apply the first rule that fits, using the deadline or date that belongs to **that action**. Work that feeds a submission, such as preparing label text for a variation, uses that submission's deadline. A deadline set for another organisation, such as a supplier's reply to a regulator, is not a Caldova deadline.

1. If a Caldova SOP sets a time limit for the action (for example *within 14 calendar days*), the internal due date is today plus that limit. Example: today 3 May plus 14 days gives 17 May.
2. If the document gives a deadline for the action (a date it must be done by, such as *submit by 10 June*), the internal due date is **14 calendar days before** that deadline. Example: a deadline of 10 June gives 27 May.
3. If there is no deadline but the document takes effect **after today**, the internal due date is **30 calendar days before** the effective date. This includes actions that must be in place *before the document takes effect*: that is the effective date, not a deadline. Example: effective 15 September gives 16 August. An effective date that is today or earlier does not count: go to rule 4.
4. If none of these apply, the internal due date is **30 calendar days after today**. Example: today 3 May gives 2 June. The approver may change it at sign-off.

If an internal due date is already in the past, flag the task as **at risk** and escalate to the Regulatory Affairs Lead.
