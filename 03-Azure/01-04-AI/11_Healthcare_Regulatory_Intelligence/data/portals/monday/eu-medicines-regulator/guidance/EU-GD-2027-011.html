Clinical trial results: plain-language summaries

This guidance explains how sponsors publish the results of completed clinical trials in the EU. It applies from 1 March 2027.

For trials that ended on or after 1 January 2026, the sponsor must publish a results summary and a plain-language summary for patients within 12 months of the end of the trial.

The plain-language summary must be written for a non-specialist reader and reviewed by at least one patient representative before publication.

Sponsors should record the publication date of both summaries in their trial register.
