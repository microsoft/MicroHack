Caldivex: new warning on liver injury

The EU medicines regulator has reviewed reports of raised liver enzymes and rare cases of serious liver injury in patients taking Caldivex tablets and the Caldivex injection pen.

Marketing authorisation holders must update the product information. Add a warning to section 4.4 (special warnings), add liver injury to section 4.8 (undesirable effects) and update the package leaflet. Doctors should check liver function before starting treatment and again after 3 months.

The marketing authorisation holder must submit a variation with the updated product information by 15 March 2027.

A Direct Healthcare Professional Communication (DHPC) must be agreed with the regulator and sent to prescribers by 1 March 2027.

Liver injury must be added as an important identified risk in the risk management plan and discussed in the next periodic safety update report (PSUR).
