Caldivex: liver injury warning for UK product information

The UK medicines regulator agrees with the EU safety review of Caldivex and liver injury.

UK marketing authorisation holders must update sections 4.4 and 4.8 of the UK Summary of Product Characteristics and the patient information leaflet. Submit the variation by 29 March 2027.

Healthcare professionals should report suspected serious liver injury through the UK adverse reaction reporting scheme. Marketing authorisation holders must report serious cases within 15 days.
