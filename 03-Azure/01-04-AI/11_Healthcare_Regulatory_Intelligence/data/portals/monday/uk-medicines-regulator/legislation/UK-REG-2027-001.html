Pharmacovigilance amendment: periodic safety report timelines

An amendment to the UK medicines regulations changes the timelines for periodic safety update reports (PSURs).

From 1 July 2027, every PSUR must be submitted within 60 days of its data lock point. The previous timelines of 70 and 90 days no longer apply.

Every UK marketing authorisation holder must update its pharmacovigilance procedures before the amendment takes effect.
