Branded medicines pricing scheme: 2027 payment rate

The UK branded medicines pricing scheme sets the payment rate that companies pay on their sales of branded medicines to the NHS.

From 1 April 2027 the payment rate is 18.5% of eligible net sales. The current rate is 15.3%.

Scheme members must update their sales forecasts and report each quarter's sales within 30 days of the quarter end.
