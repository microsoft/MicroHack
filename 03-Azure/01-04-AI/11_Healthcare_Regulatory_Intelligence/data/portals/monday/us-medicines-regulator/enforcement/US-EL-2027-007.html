Warning letter to contract manufacturer CMO-17

The US medicines regulator inspected contract manufacturer CMO-17, a fill-finish site for prefilled injection pens, and found significant deviations from current good manufacturing practice.

Finding 1: investigations into particles found in prefilled injection pens were not completed and the root cause was not identified.

Finding 2: cleaning validation of the pen filling line was incomplete.

CMO-17 must reply within 15 working days with a corrective action plan. Companies whose products are filled at CMO-17 should assess the impact on product quality and supply.
