Nitrosamine impurities: acceptable intake limits

This rule sets acceptable intake limits for nitrosamine impurities in medicines sold in the US.

Manufacturers must complete a nitrosamine risk assessment for each tablet and capsule product and confirm, by testing, that impurity levels are below the limit by 1 August 2027.

A product that exceeds the limit must not be released for sale after 1 August 2027 unless the regulator has agreed a remediation plan.
