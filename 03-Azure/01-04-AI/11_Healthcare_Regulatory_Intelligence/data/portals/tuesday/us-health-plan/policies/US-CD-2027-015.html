Coverage policy: preventive migraine medicines

The health plan has reviewed its coverage of medicines that prevent migraine. This revision replaces the version published on 11 January 2027.

From 1 April 2027, Caldivex tablets move from tier 2 to tier 3 of the formulary. Patients will pay a higher share of the cost.

Prior authorisation will require documented failure of one other preventive treatment.

Manufacturers may submit new clinical or economic evidence by 1 March 2027 for the plan to consider.
