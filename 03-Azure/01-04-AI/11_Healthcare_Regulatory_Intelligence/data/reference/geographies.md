## Kinds of publication

| Type | What it is | Usually binding? |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation | Law or a formal rule | Yes |
| Guidance | The regulator's explanation of what it expects | Expected, not law |
| Safety alert | Instruction after a safety review | Yes, for the named products |
| Enforcement letter | Formal finding against a named company or site | Yes, for the named site |
| Coverage decision | A payer's rules for paying for a medicine | Commercial, not law |
| Pricing rule | Price or payment terms set by a payer or scheme | Yes, for scheme members |
