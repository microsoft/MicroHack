# Finish: Estimate the impact and clean up

**[← Previous](challenge-08.md)** - [Home](../README.md)

## 🎯 Objective

Turn what you built into a short business case for your company, and leave your lab tidy.

## ✅ Tasks

### Part A: Estimate the impact (10 min)

Use your industry's numbers from the supervisor digest (Challenge 6) and these assumptions. Change any you disagree with; the point is to reason about it, not to get one right answer.

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Contacts per year at your company | 1,200,000 |
| Share of contacts that are routine (answer from an article, check status, book a slot) | Use the share you saw in your week of contacts |
| Share of routine contacts the agent resolves without a person | 50% |
| Cost of a contact handled by a person | 6 pounds |
| Cost of a contact handled by the agent | 0.60 pounds |
| Share of calls checked by quality today | 2% |

1. How many contacts a year would the agent resolve, and what would that save?
2. Quality checks: today 2% of calls are reviewed. With post-contact analytics every call gets a scorecard. In your week of contacts, how many missed identity checks would a 2% sample probably have caught?
3. Handoffs: when a vulnerable customer is handed over with a summary, what does the customer no longer have to do? What is that worth to your company's reputation, and to the regulator?
4. What would you need to see in the evaluation (Challenge 7) before you'd put the voice agent live for real customers?

Write your answers as three or four sentences you could say to your company's head of customer service.

### Part B: Clean up (5 min)

1. In the Foundry portal, delete the agents you created if your facilitator asks you to: `contact-voice-agent`, `contact-chat-agent`, `agent-assist` and `supervisor-insights`.
2. Stop the chat page in Codespaces (Ctrl+C) and delete the codespace from [github.com/codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces) if you made one.
3. Leave the resource group in place. Your facilitator removes the lab environment.

## ✔️ Success criteria

- [ ] You estimated contacts resolved and the saving for your company, with your assumptions written down.
- [ ] You can explain the value of checking every call instead of a sample, using your week of contacts.
- [ ] You can name what you'd need to see before putting the agent in front of real customers.

## 🎉 Well done

You built a contact center that answers from your company's knowledge, takes real action, protects customers, hands over to people with full context, learns from every call and is measured before it goes live.
