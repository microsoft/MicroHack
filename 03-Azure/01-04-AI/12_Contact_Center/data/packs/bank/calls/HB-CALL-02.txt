# HB-CALL-02: Parcel fee text scam

Customer voice: female

Agent: Thanks for calling Harbourline Bank. How can I help today?
Customer: I paid a parcel fee from a text and now someone's tried to take three hundred pounds from my account. I'm panicking.
Agent: I'm sorry, you did the right thing calling us. Can I take your account number and postcode?
Customer: H B five zero zero three zero one, Z Z three, six L A.
Agent: Thank you, Natalie. That text is a known scam. I'm putting you through to a fraud specialist straight away so they can protect your account.
Customer: Will I get my money back?
Agent: The fraud team will look at that with you now. I've passed on everything you told me.
