# KB-HB-004: Service levels and opening hours

Harbourline Bank. Fictional training article.

[KB-HB-004 p1] Phone and chat lines are open 8am to 8pm every day for card and fraud matters, and 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday for mortgages. The virtual assistant is available at any time.

[KB-HB-004 p2] Cases are answered within 1 working day when priority is high and within 5 working days when priority is normal. Suspected fraud is always high priority.

[KB-HB-004 p3] Mortgage adviser calls last 45 minutes and are booked on weekdays.
