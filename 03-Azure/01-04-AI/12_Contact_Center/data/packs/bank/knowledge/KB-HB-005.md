# KB-HB-005: Lost or stolen cards

Harbourline Bank. Fictional training article.

[KB-HB-005 p1] Customers can freeze a card instantly in the app. Freezing is reversible; reporting the card lost or stolen cancels it permanently.

[KB-HB-005 p2] A replacement card arrives within 3 to 5 working days. The card number changes, so customers must update any regular card payments.

[KB-HB-005 p3] Do not ask for the full card number, PIN, passcode or one-time passcode at any point.
