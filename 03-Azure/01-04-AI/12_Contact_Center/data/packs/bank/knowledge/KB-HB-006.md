# KB-HB-006: Disputing a card payment

Harbourline Bank. Fictional training article.

[KB-HB-006 p1] Customers can dispute a card payment within 120 days of the transaction if goods did not arrive, were not as described, or a payment was taken twice.

[KB-HB-006 p2] Ask the customer to contact the merchant first. If that fails, open a case with category card dispute, the amount, the date and the merchant name.

[KB-HB-006 p3] A temporary credit may be added while the dispute is investigated. Investigations take up to 30 days.
