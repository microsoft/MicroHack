# KB-HB-007: Suspected fraud and scams

Harbourline Bank. Fictional training article.

[KB-HB-007 p1] Harbourline Bank will never ask a customer to move money to a safe account, share a one-time passcode or give their PIN. Anyone asking for these is a criminal.

[KB-HB-007 p2] If a customer thinks they have been scammed or are being pressured to pay, hand them to a human fraud adviser straight away. Speed matters, because money can sometimes be recovered.

[KB-HB-007 p3] Common scams this month include a text claiming a parcel delivery fee is due, which links to a fake payment page.
