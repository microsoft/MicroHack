# KB-HB-008: Mortgage adviser calls

Harbourline Bank. Fictional training article.

[KB-HB-008 p1] Customers who want to remortgage, borrow more or move home with a mortgage can book a 45-minute call with a mortgage adviser.

[KB-HB-008 p2] Book the first suitable slot and remind the customer to have their latest payslips and bank statements to hand.

[KB-HB-008 p3] Advisers can only give mortgage advice; the virtual assistant does not recommend products or rates.
