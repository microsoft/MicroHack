# KB-LE-002: Supporting customers who may be vulnerable

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-002 p1] A customer may be vulnerable because of health, a life event such as bereavement, money difficulties or low confidence with technology. Signs include the customer saying so, sounding distressed, mentioning medical equipment that needs power or a connection, or struggling to follow the conversation.

[KB-LE-002 p2] When you notice these signs, slow down, check the customer is comfortable to continue, and do not push for a decision.

[KB-LE-002 p3] Hand the customer to a human adviser when they ask for a person, are distressed, mention a risk to their safety or health, or when the issue needs a judgement you cannot make. Record the reason and a short summary so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

[KB-LE-002 p4] Never promise an outcome on behalf of the human team. Tell the customer what will happen next and how long it usually takes.
