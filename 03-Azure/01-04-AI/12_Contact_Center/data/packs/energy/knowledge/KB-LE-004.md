# KB-LE-004: Service levels and opening hours

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-004 p1] Phone and chat lines are open 8am to 8pm Monday to Friday and 9am to 1pm on Saturday. The virtual assistant is available at any time and hands over to a person during opening hours.

[KB-LE-004 p2] Cases are answered within 1 working day when priority is high and within 5 working days when priority is normal.

[KB-LE-004 p3] Smart meter installations take place on weekdays in 2-hour arrival windows between 8am and 5pm.
