# KB-LE-005: Bills, estimated readings and meter readings

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-005 p1] If we do not receive a meter reading, the bill is based on an estimate. Estimated bills are marked with the letter E next to the reading.

[KB-LE-005 p2] Customers can give a reading at any time. A new bill based on the actual reading is produced within 3 working days, and any overcharge is credited to the account.

[KB-LE-005 p3] When a bill is much higher than usual, first check whether it is based on an estimate, then ask the customer for a reading.
