# KB-LE-006: Smart meters and installation appointments

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-006 p1] Smart meters are installed free of charge and send readings automatically, so bills are no longer estimated.

[KB-LE-006 p2] Book the first suitable installation slot in the customer's area. Installation takes about 2 hours and the power is off for around 30 minutes.

[KB-LE-006 p3] Someone aged 18 or over must be at home and the meters must be easy to reach. Appointments can be moved free of charge up to 48 hours before the window.

[KB-LE-006 p4] Customers who use powered medical equipment are offered an installation plan that keeps the power on, arranged by a human adviser.
