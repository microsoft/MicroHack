# KB-LE-007: Payment difficulties and the priority services register

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-007 p1] Customers who are struggling to pay can agree a payment plan that spreads a debt over up to 12 months. Open a case with category payment arrangement.

[KB-LE-007 p2] The priority services register gives extra help, such as advance warning of planned power cuts, to customers who are older, disabled, chronically ill, have young children or use powered medical equipment. Offer it whenever these needs come up.

[KB-LE-007 p3] Prepayment customers who cannot top up can be given emergency credit of 15 pounds by a human adviser.
