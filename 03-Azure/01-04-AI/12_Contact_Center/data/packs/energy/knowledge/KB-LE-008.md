# KB-LE-008: Power cuts

Lumora Energy. Fictional training article.

[KB-LE-008 p1] Power cuts are handled by the local network operator, not by Lumora Energy. Give the customer the free national power cut number, 105.

[KB-LE-008 p2] Known supply problems in an area are listed as service notices with an estimated fix time.

[KB-LE-008 p3] Customers who use powered medical equipment and have lost power must be handed to a human adviser straight away.
