# KB-NI-003: Complaints and escalation

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-003 p1] A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, whether or not the customer uses the word complaint. Acknowledge it, apologise for the experience and say what you can do now.

[KB-NI-003 p2] If you can fix the problem during the contact, do so and confirm the result. Otherwise open a case with a clear summary, the customer's preferred outcome and a priority.

[KB-NI-003 p3] Priority is high when the customer is vulnerable, has no service, or has already contacted us about the same issue. Otherwise it is normal.

[KB-NI-003 p4] The customer receives a case number. Response times for cases are in the service levels article.
