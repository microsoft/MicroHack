# KB-NI-004: Service levels and opening hours

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-004 p1] The claims line is open 24 hours a day for emergencies. Other enquiries are handled 8am to 8pm Monday to Saturday.

[KB-NI-004 p2] Cases are answered within 1 working day when priority is high and within 5 working days when priority is normal.

[KB-NI-004 p3] Claims assessor visits are booked on weekdays in 2-hour arrival windows between 8am and 5pm.
