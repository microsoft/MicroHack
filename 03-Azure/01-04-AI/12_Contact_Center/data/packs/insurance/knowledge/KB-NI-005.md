# KB-NI-005: Making a home claim

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-005 p1] To start a home claim, record what happened, when it happened, what was damaged and whether anyone is hurt or the home is unsafe.

[KB-NI-005 p2] The policy excess is 250 pounds for home claims unless the policy schedule shows a different amount. The excess is taken off the settlement.

[KB-NI-005 p3] Customers should keep damaged items and take photos, but must not throw anything away before the claim is assessed, unless it is unsafe or unhygienic.
