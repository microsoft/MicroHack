# KB-NI-006: Storm and escape of water damage

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-006 p1] If the home is unsafe or water is still escaping, the customer should turn off the water at the stopcock and, if it is safe, switch off the electricity.

[KB-NI-006 p2] Customers can arrange emergency temporary repairs up to 500 pounds, such as covering a damaged roof, without waiting for approval. They should keep the receipts.

[KB-NI-006 p3] After a major storm, claims are handled in order of urgency. Homes that are unsafe to live in come first.

[KB-NI-006 p4] If the customer cannot stay in the home, alternative accommodation is covered for home buildings policies. Hand these customers to a human adviser.
