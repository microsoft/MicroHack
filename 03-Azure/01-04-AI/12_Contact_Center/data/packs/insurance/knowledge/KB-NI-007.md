# KB-NI-007: Motor claims and courtesy cars

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-007 p1] After an accident, record the date, place, the other driver's details and whether anyone was injured. If anyone was injured, hand the customer to a human adviser.

[KB-NI-007 p2] Comprehensive motor policies include a small courtesy car while the customer's car is repaired at an approved garage. Third party policies do not.

[KB-NI-007 p3] Customers must not admit fault at the scene or agree to settle privately with the other driver.
