# KB-NI-008: Claims assessor visits

Northmere Insurance. Fictional training article.

[KB-NI-008 p1] For larger home claims, a claims assessor visits to inspect the damage. Book the first suitable slot in the customer's area.

[KB-NI-008 p2] The customer should have photos, receipts and the policy number ready. The visit takes about an hour.

[KB-NI-008 p3] Visits can be moved free of charge up to 24 hours before the window.
