# KB-PP-001: Checking who you are speaking to

Parcelpine. Fictional training article.

[KB-PP-001 p1] Before giving out or changing anything on an account, check the caller's identity. Ask for the account number and the postcode on the account. Both must match the customer record.

[KB-PP-001 p2] If the details do not match, say so politely and do not share any account information. Offer to help with general questions instead, or ask the caller to check the details on a recent bill or letter.

[KB-PP-001 p3] Never read out a full account number, date of birth or payment card number. You may confirm the last two characters of an account number.

[KB-PP-001 p4] General information that is the same for every customer, such as opening hours or how a process works, can be given without checking identity.
