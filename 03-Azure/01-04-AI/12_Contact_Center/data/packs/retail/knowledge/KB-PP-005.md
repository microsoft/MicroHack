# KB-PP-005: Delivery times and tracking

Parcelpine. Fictional training article.

[KB-PP-005 p1] Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Next-day delivery arrives the next working day for orders placed before 8pm. Large items arrive within 10 working days.

[KB-PP-005 p2] Every order has a tracking link in the confirmation email. Known delivery delays in an area are listed as service notices.

[KB-PP-005 p3] If an order is more than 5 working days late, the customer can choose a full refund or a free redelivery. Parcelpine Plus members also get their delivery fee refunded.
