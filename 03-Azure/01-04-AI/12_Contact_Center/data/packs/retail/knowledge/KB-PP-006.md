# KB-PP-006: Returns and refunds

Parcelpine. Fictional training article.

[KB-PP-006 p1] Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if they are unused and in their original packaging.

[KB-PP-006 p2] Small items are returned free at any drop-off point. Large items are collected free of charge; book a returns collection.

[KB-PP-006 p3] Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return arriving at the warehouse.
