# KB-PP-007: Damaged or faulty items

Parcelpine. Fictional training article.

[KB-PP-007 p1] If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault within 12 months, the customer can choose a replacement, a repair or a refund.

[KB-PP-007 p2] Ask the customer for a photo of the damage, uploaded through the order page. Open a case with category damaged item.

[KB-PP-007 p3] Customers do not need to return a damaged item worth less than 20 pounds; the refund is made without a return.
