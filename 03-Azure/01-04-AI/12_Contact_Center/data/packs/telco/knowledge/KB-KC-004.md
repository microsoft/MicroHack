# KB-KC-004: Service levels and opening hours

Kestrel Connect. Fictional training article.

[KB-KC-004 p1] Phone and chat lines are open 8am to 8pm Monday to Saturday. The virtual assistant is available at any time and hands over to a person during opening hours.

[KB-KC-004 p2] Cases are answered within 1 working day when priority is high and within 5 working days when priority is normal.

[KB-KC-004 p3] Engineer visits are booked in 2-hour arrival windows from 8am to 5pm on weekdays.
