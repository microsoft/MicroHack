# KB-KC-005: Broadband outages and faults

Kestrel Connect. Fictional training article.

[KB-KC-005 p1] First check whether there is a known outage in the customer's postcode area. Known outages are listed with an estimated fix time; tell the customer the estimate and do not book an engineer for a known outage.

[KB-KC-005 p2] If there is no known outage, ask the customer to restart the router by switching it off for 30 seconds. If the light is still red after 5 minutes, book an engineer visit.

[KB-KC-005 p3] When broadband has been down for more than 2 full working days, compensation of 8 pounds per day is added to the next bill automatically. The customer does not need to claim it.

[KB-KC-005 p4] Customers who rely on broadband for a telecare or medical alarm must be told about the free 4G backup hub and handed to a human adviser straight away.
