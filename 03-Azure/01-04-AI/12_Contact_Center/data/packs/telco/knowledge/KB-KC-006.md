# KB-KC-006: Engineer appointments

Kestrel Connect. Fictional training article.

[KB-KC-006 p1] Engineer visits are free for faults on the Kestrel Connect network. Book the first suitable slot in the customer's area and confirm the date and arrival window.

[KB-KC-006 p2] Someone aged 18 or over must be at home for the whole arrival window.

[KB-KC-006 p3] Appointments can be moved or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the window starts. A missed appointment without notice costs 40 pounds.
