# KB-KC-007: Bills and payment arrangements

Kestrel Connect. Fictional training article.

[KB-KC-007 p1] Bills are issued on the same day each month. The first bill after joining or changing plan includes a part-month charge, so it is usually higher than later bills.

[KB-KC-007 p2] A late payment fee of 5 pounds applies if a payment is more than 14 days late.

[KB-KC-007 p3] Customers in financial difficulty can spread an overdue balance over up to 3 months with no extra fee. Open a case with category payment arrangement and the agreed amounts.

[KB-KC-007 p4] Never take card details through the virtual assistant. Customers pay through the app, the website or a human adviser.
