# KB-KC-008: Moving home and ending a contract

Kestrel Connect. Fictional training article.

[KB-KC-008 p1] Customers moving home keep their plan if Kestrel Connect covers the new address. Ask for at least 14 days' notice so the line can be ready on moving day.

[KB-KC-008 p2] Customers who end a contract early pay the remaining monthly charges up to the end of the minimum term, unless the reason is a fault that we could not fix within 30 days.

[KB-KC-008 p3] To leave at the end of the minimum term, customers give 30 days' notice. Open a case with category cancellation.
