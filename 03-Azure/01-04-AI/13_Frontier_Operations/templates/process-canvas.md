# Process canvas: your own next process

Fill this in as a team in the Finish. One canvas per process. Keep it to what you know today; it's a first draft, not a business case.

| How long does one run take today (people × hours)? | |
| What triggers it? | |
| What does "done" look like, and who receives it? | |

## 2. Is it ready for an agent?

Tick what applies. Three or more ticks usually means a good candidate.

- [ ] People move data between files, emails and systems.
- [ ] The rules are written down somewhere, or one expert knows them.
- [ ] Most of the time goes on preparing, matching or chasing, not on deciding.
- [ ] Exceptions follow a pattern you could explain to a new joiner.
- [ ] At least one system has no API, and people type into its web page.
- [ ] A person already approves the result before it leaves the team.
